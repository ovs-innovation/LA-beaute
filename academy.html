About us

A salon that also trains artists

LA BEAUTÉ Academy sits on the same floor as our Delhi salon. Mentors still take paying clients. Students practise where appointments happen — with the same hygiene, timing and guest standard.

Weekend and evening batches are kept for college students and working professionals. A photo session is built into longer programmes so you leave with images you can show a salon or a bride.

Learn from people who still work — then take that method into a chair of your own.
