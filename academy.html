The institute

A working salon that runs as an academy

LA BEAUTÉ Academy is an in-person makeup school on our Delhi salon floor. Theory is taught first. Then you practise on live models under a mentor who still takes paying clients.

Batches are capped so every student is corrected, not left to copy a demo. Weekend and evening seats are reserved for working professionals and college students.

Longer programmes include a directed portfolio session and a practical assessment before the certificate is issued.

Academic structure

How a programme is taught

Every course follows the same academic order: theory, supervised practice, live models, then assessment.

  1. 01 · TheorySkin science, colour, sanitation and product language.
  2. 02 · DrillsRepeated technique on models until the method is consistent.
  3. 03 · Floor hoursTimed looks under the same standard as salon appointments.
  4. 04 · AssessmentPractical exam, kit review and a finishing look for the file.
