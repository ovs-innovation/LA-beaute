Jyoti Singh

Celebrity, PMU makeup artist & educator

A professional educator for you — and for your service.

The face behind LA BEAUTÉ. Jyoti Singh is a celebrity and PMU makeup artist and educator — bridal, party, editorial, and permanent makeup — who still sits with every look before she teaches it.

The atelier is built the way she works: unhurried, precise, and personal. Guests come for a makeover. Students train beside the same artist and educator who takes the bookings.
